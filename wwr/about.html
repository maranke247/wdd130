Whitewater Rafting Co.

Our mission is to provide safe, unforgettable outdoor river adventures for families, beginners, and thrill-seekers alike. Come experience the rush of wild rivers with our certified guides.

History

Founded over two decades ago by passionate river guides, Whitewater Rafting Co. started with two rafts and a dream to share the beauty of untamed canyons with outdoor lovers.

Today, we lead thousands of guests every season across scenic mountain waterways while maintaining a 100% commitment to river conservation and guest safety.
